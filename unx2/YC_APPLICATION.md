# UNX2 — YC APPLICATION CORE

## Company
UNX2 — AI Business Operating System.

## What does the company make?
UNX2 helps small businesses identify repetitive operational work and deploy AI agents to automate those workflows while measuring business impact.

## Initial wedge
Lead qualification, research, email preparation, CRM updates and reporting.

## Product
UNX2 provides a diagnostic layer, agent registry, workflow execution, telemetry and ROI measurement. LÉVIATHAN is the underlying orchestration layer.

## Important evidence rule
Do not claim users, revenue, retention, savings or product-market fit until they are measured with real customers.

## Metrics to instrument
- signup
- activation
- first diagnostic
- first workflow
- successful workflow
- human approval
- hours saved
- customer-reported value
- paid conversion
- MRR
- retention
- AI cost / task

## YC target
Build evidence before applying: real users, real workflows, real payments and clear week-over-week learning.
